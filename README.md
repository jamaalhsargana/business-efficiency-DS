In this report we will be outlining two specific use cases where data science can provide impactful 
solutions: 
 Customer Churn Prediction 
Retaining customers is significantly more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. Predicting 
customer churn using tabular data, including historical booking patterns, engagement 
metrics, and satisfaction scores, can help identify at-risk customers and implement targeted 
retention strategies. By the research we will try to understand the factors driving churn, the 
company can improve customer loyalty, reduce attrition, and enhance the overall customer 
experience. 
 Time Series Forecasting for Hotel and Flight Bookings 
Accurate forecasting of hotel and flight bookings is essential for optimizing resource 
allocation, managing inventory, and maximizing revenue. Time series analysis on historical 
booking data is crucial in predicting future demand, enabling effective pricing strategies, 
efficient staffing, and tailored marketing campaigns. By identifying patterns such as trends 
and seasonality, the business would be prepared for peak periods and minimize the impact 
of low-demand seasons. 
The bases of this analysis in each use case we will utilize synthetic datasets resembling real-world 
scenarios, enabling the application of advanced machine learning and statistical techniques. In this 
report we will emphasis on aligning data science methods with business objectives and with 
research try to demonstrate how predictive insights can drive better decision-making, ultimately 
transforming our organisation into a more data-driven and efficient company.
