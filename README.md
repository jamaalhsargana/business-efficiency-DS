Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Strategy
Built an XGBoost model to predict which customers were likely to leave, achieving strong performance metrics (accuracy, precision, recall). The analysis revealed:
•	LastActivityDays was the strongest churn predictor.
•	Loyalty program members had significantly lower churn and higher satisfaction.
•	Rural customers and those aged 51–60 showed the highest attrition rates.
Impact: This helped proactively target at-risk customers with tailored retention campaigns, enhance loyalty programs, and improve complaint resolution directly boosting customer lifetime value.
Time Series Forecasting for Hotel & Flight Demand
Using ARIMA models, I forecasted monthly demand with confidence intervals, identifying clear seasonal peaks and upward trends. I was also able to run scenario analyses like +50% or -25% demand shifts to prepare for possible volatility.
Impact: These results would enable dynamic pricing, optimized staffing, efficient fleet scheduling, and strategic resource allocation, reducing costs and maximizing revenue during peak periods.
